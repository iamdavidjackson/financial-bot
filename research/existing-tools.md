# Existing Financial Advisor Tools

Use this file to record findings on existing tools, products, and services in the financial advisor space.

## Tool 1

**Name:**  Danelfin
**URL:**  https://danelfin.com/

**Features Included:**
- Stock price chart with time frame filters for 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 5 years, all time, or a custom date range.
- Current stock data including market cap, volume, dividends, 52-week high/low, and average volume.
- Simple stock rating out of 10 with a buy or sell recommendation.
- Sentiment input that considers what other analysts think about the stock.
- Pro version provides recommended stop-loss and take-profit points.
- Portfolios feature for managing owned stocks in the UI, including sell information when appropriate.

**Missing Features:**
- Does not include news or press releases in the UI.
- No LLM chat feature; all displayed data must be interpreted from content already shown on the page.

**UI Description:**
- Clean, high-contrast interface with light grey palette and an easy-to-read sans serif font.
- Uses green to indicate positive information and red to indicate negative information.
- Website-style layout with header, footer, and side navigation.

**Notes:**
- Strategy example: Danelfin describes a U.S.-listed "Best Stocks" strategy that starts with an equally weighted 25-stock portfolio. It filters for companies with a Buy Track Record, Low Risk score of at least 5/10, and average 3-month trading volume above 100K.
- The strategy then ranks sectors by the average AI Score of eligible stocks, selects the top five sectors, and chooses the five highest AI Score stocks within each selected sector. If a sector lacks enough Buy Track Record stocks, remaining positions are filled using the same criteria without that condition.
- Rebalancing occurs every 3 months to replace stocks that no longer qualify, with a final rebalance every 12 months to restore equal weighting across all holdings.
- Source: https://danelfin.com/best-stock-investment-strategy

## Tool 2

**Name:**  Kavout
**URL:**  https://www.kavout.com/

**Features Included:**
- Stock price chart with time frame filters for 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 5 years, and all time.
- Stock-related news shown in the UI.
- Technical analysis based on multiple trading perspectives.
- Gauge-style charts showing recommendation levels: strong sell, sell, neutral, buy, and strong buy.
- Portfolio feature for managing tracked or owned stocks.

**Missing Features:**
- Brokerage connections are read-only, even though it supports 30+ brokerages.
- Does not allow users to place trades inside the app.
- Does not automatically execute trades for users.

**UI Description:**
- Primary navigation is on the left.
- Main content area is focused around a chat interface.
- Below the chat area, stock ticker cards show the most popular stocks.

## Tool 3

**Name:**  Fiscal.ai
**URL:**  https://fiscal.ai/

**Features Included:**
- Text description of the company, including the CEO name.
- Company statistics including number of employees and many other financial fields.
- Stock price chart with expected time frame filters.
- Ability to download chart data.
- Ability to choose metrics to compare on a chart.

**Missing Features:**
- Does not appear to provide a clear buy or sell recommendation for a stock.
- Does not appear to provide technical information about how its algorithms work.

**UI Description:**
- Dark user interface.
- Uses left-side navigation.
- Feels more like a dashboard than a traditional website.
